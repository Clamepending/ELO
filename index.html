Life Rating

Pick photos from your gallery, Google Photos, or a Google Takeout, Instagram or X export. A CLIP model running in this browser tags each one; nothing is uploaded. Ratings are Elo-scaled (1500 = reference median, ±95% interval). Click any score to break it down.

Open Google Photos picker or drop images here · Takeout .json sidecars are used for dates and GPS · Google Photos imports have no location (Google strips it) and are relayed through this site without being stored Instagram: Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information → JSON format. X: Settings → Your account → Download an archive. Choose the downloaded .zip (or its unzipped folder); only your own posts and stories are used, never messages.
